Candidate trigger logic found in your files

The framework says trigger logic belongs in the canonical registry, so none of these is the rule. They are the three versions that exist, so you can choose, combine or replace them. I have not written any into the app.

Option A
Two-axis threshold
Bone axis ≥ 40
AND Muscle axis ≥ 40

Flags severity "Critical". Scale is the scorecard's raw 0–100 risk score where higher is worse, so it must never be shown beside the MM Health Score.

Source: GMMBB Axis Assessment Scorecard workbook. Its sample rows exceed their own 100-point maximum, so treat the thresholds as unvalidated.

Option B
Flag count within an axis
3 or more YES flags
within a single axis

A general compound-risk rule that applies to every axis, regardless of total score. Would need a bone-and-muscle specific version to serve CP-04.

Source: GMMBB Catabolic Risk Assessment form and the SaaS development skill.

Option C
Construct-level, per canon
bone_density low
AND a muscle-side construct low

Follows the framework's rule that constructs are scored individually. Thresholds are not defined anywhere yet; CCRAF Section C already scores DEXA T-score bands that could supply the bone input.

Source: framework.md constructs rule; CCRAF Section C (8 questions, 75 points).

  2. Choose the trigger rule.Option A, B, C, or a combination, with the thresholds you want clinically.
  3. Choose the primary home.Pillar 1, where the studies are filed, or Pillar 4, where the bone_density construct is scored. The other becomes a named bridge.
  4. Settle the figures.Confirm the fracture multiplier and its correct first author, and decide what happens to the "3×" and "doubling" wording.
  5. Decide the 7–14 day claim.Trace a study for it, or keep it out of all CP-04 content.
